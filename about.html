Who We Are

InvoVerse Holdings was founded in 2025 by Sbongakonke Sithole, who serves as the sole Director and Founder. The company focuses on delivering modern digital solutions including websites, systems, and applications.

We are a .NET-driven company, building reliable and scalable systems, with future plans to expand into additional frameworks and technologies to stay ahead in the evolving tech industry.

Our headquarters are based in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, with a growing representative presence across multiple provinces to support collaboration with SMEs.
